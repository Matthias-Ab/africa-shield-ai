AFRICA SHIELD AI — FRENCH TRANSLATION REVIEW
===============================================

Status: AI-written, NEVER reviewed by a native/fluent French speaker.
Added 2026-08-17, to reach more Francophone African countries.
Source file: backend/app/models/translations.py (French block, near the
bottom of ALERT_TEMPLATES).
Used for: SMS alerts, USSD "check flood risk" reply, and voice call
text-to-speech (spoken aloud when someone answers a voice alert).

Demo city used below: Kinshasa, DRC (our only sample city currently
mapped to French — DRC's actual official language).

City names are NOT renamed for French in this app — Kinshasa, Dakar,
Abidjan, etc. are already their French names, so no localization
mapping was needed (unlike Arabic/Somali, where the city name changes).

--------------------------------------------------
1. HIGH RISK
--------------------------------------------------
French (as sent):
Le risque d'inondation est ÉLEVÉ à Kinshasa. Déplacez-vous vers un
terrain élevé et évitez les berges de la rivière.

Intended meaning (English gloss, for reference only — not the source
text, just what we think it says):
"Flood risk is HIGH in Kinshasa. Move to higher ground and avoid
riverbanks."

--------------------------------------------------
2. MEDIUM RISK
--------------------------------------------------
French (as sent):
Le risque d'inondation est MOYEN à Kinshasa. Restez vigilant et suivez
les mises à jour locales.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is MEDIUM in Kinshasa. Stay alert and follow local updates."

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOW RISK
--------------------------------------------------
French (as sent):
Le risque d'inondation est FAIBLE à Kinshasa. Aucune action n'est
nécessaire pour le moment.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is LOW in Kinshasa. No action is necessary at this time."


WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
======================
1. Is the grammar/wording natural for each of the 3 messages above?
2. Do "ÉLEVÉ" / "MOYEN" / "FAIBLE" read clearly as HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
   risk levels, or is there a more natural way to phrase the severity?
3. Is the tone right for an emergency/safety alert?
4. This same wording will be reused for any other French-mapped country
   (Senegal, Mali, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Niger, Chad, Burkina Faso,
   Benin, Togo, Guinea, Gabon, Madagascar, Central African Republic,
   Rwanda, Burundi) — flag if anything reads as too
   France-French/not-regionally-neutral for West/Central African French.
