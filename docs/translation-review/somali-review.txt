AFRICA SHIELD AI — SOMALI TRANSLATION REVIEW
===============================================

STATUS: CONFIRMED CORRECT by a native Somali speaker (2026-08-17).
Source file: backend/app/models/translations.py (lines ~127-131).
Used for: SMS alerts, USSD "check flood risk" reply, and voice call
text-to-speech (spoken aloud when someone answers a voice alert).

Demo city used below: Mogadishu, Somalia (our only sample city mapped
to Somali).

UPDATE (2026-08-17): per this review, the city name is now written using
the Somali name ("Muqdisho") instead of the English name ("Mogadishu")
inside the Somali sentence. This file has been updated to show the
current, corrected live output.

--------------------------------------------------
1. HIGH RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Somali (as sent):
Khatarta daadka ee Muqdisho waa SARE. U guuri meel sare kana fogow
xeebaha webiga.

Intended meaning (English gloss, for reference only):
"The flood risk in Mogadishu is HIGH. Move to a high place and stay
away from the riverbanks."

--------------------------------------------------
2. MEDIUM RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Somali (as sent):
Khatarta daadka ee Muqdisho waa DHEXDHEXAAD. La soco warbixinnaha
maxaliga ah.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"The flood risk in Mogadishu is MODERATE. Follow local updates."

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOW RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Somali (as sent):
Khatarta daadka ee Muqdisho waa HOOSE. Wax tallaabo lagama qaadanayo
hadda.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"The flood risk in Mogadishu is LOW. No action is being taken at this
time."


REVIEW OUTCOME
================
All 3 messages confirmed correct. City name changed to the Somali name
("Muqdisho") per reviewer feedback.
