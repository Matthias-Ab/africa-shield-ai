AFRICA SHIELD AI — AMHARIC TRANSLATION REVIEW
================================================

Status: AI-written, NEVER reviewed by a native/fluent Amharic speaker.
Added 2026-08-17, to match the African Union's official languages
(Ethiopia's official language).

**THIS IS THE LEAST CONFIDENT DRAFT OF ALL SEVEN LANGUAGES IN THIS
APP.** Amharic uses a different script (Ge'ez) and is linguistically
further from the team's other languages than French/Portuguese are from
English or from each other. Treat this one as most likely to need real
correction, not just a polish pass.

Source file: backend/app/models/translations.py (Amharic block in
ALERT_TEMPLATES).
Used for: SMS alerts, USSD "check flood risk" reply, and voice call
text-to-speech (spoken aloud when someone answers a voice alert).

IMPORTANT CONTEXT: no city in Ethiopia is currently one of our 9 sample
cities in app/data/regions.json, so this language is NOT exercised by
the live dashboard (GET /api/regions) today — only reachable by manually
calling POST /api/risk-check with an Ethiopian location. The team is
deciding whether to add an Ethiopian sample city (e.g. Addis Ababa) to
actually demo this. Demo city used below (Addis Ababa) is illustrative,
not necessarily final.

City name: "Addis Ababa" is localized to "አዲስ አበባ" (its Amharic name,
written in Ge'ez script) inside the Amharic sentence — please also
confirm this spelling/name is correct.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
======================
1. Is the grammar correct and natural for each of the 3 messages above?
   (We expect this is the most likely of all 7 languages to have real

2. Do the high/medium/low severity words read clearly as distinct risk

3. Is "አዲስ አበባ" the correct/expected way to write "Addis Ababa"?
