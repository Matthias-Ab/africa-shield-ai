AFRICA SHIELD AI — PORTUGUESE TRANSLATION REVIEW
===================================================

Status: AI-written, NEVER reviewed by a native/fluent Portuguese speaker.
Added 2026-08-17, to match the African Union's official languages and
fix a real bug: Mozambique/Maputo had been incorrectly defaulting to
English (Portuguese is Mozambique's actual official language).
Source file: backend/app/models/translations.py (Portuguese block in
ALERT_TEMPLATES).
Used for: SMS alerts, USSD "check flood risk" reply, and voice call
text-to-speech (spoken aloud when someone answers a voice alert).

Demo city used below: Maputo, Mozambique — one of our 9 sample cities,
and the team's most real-data-validated city (a real flood event was
confirmed there in our Dec 2025-Jan 2026 real-data investigation). This
is a high-stakes one to get right.

City names are NOT renamed for Portuguese in this app — "Maputo" is
already its Portuguese name, so no localization mapping was needed
(unlike Arabic/Somali/Amharic, where the city name changes).

--------------------------------------------------
1. HIGH RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Portuguese (as sent):
O risco de inundação é ALTO em Maputo. Desloque-se para um terreno mais
elevado e evite as margens do rio.

Intended meaning (English gloss, for reference only — not the source
text, just what we think it says):
"Flood risk is HIGH in Maputo. Move to higher ground and avoid
riverbanks."

--------------------------------------------------
2. MEDIUM RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Portuguese (as sent):
O risco de inundação é MÉDIO em Maputo. Mantenha-se alerta e siga as
atualizações locais.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is MEDIUM in Maputo. Stay alert and follow local updates."

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOW RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Portuguese (as sent):
O risco de inundação é BAIXO em Maputo. Nenhuma ação é necessária neste
momento.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is LOW in Maputo. No action is necessary at this time."


WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
======================
1. Is the grammar/wording natural for each of the 3 messages above?
2. Do "ALTO" / "MÉDIO" / "BAIXO" read clearly as HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
   risk levels, or is there a more natural way to phrase the severity?
3. Is the tone right for an emergency/safety alert?
4. This same wording will be reused for any other Portuguese-mapped
   country (Angola, Guinea-Bissau, Cabo Verde, São Tomé and Príncipe,
   Equatorial Guinea) — flag if anything reads as too
   European-Portuguese/not-regionally-neutral for African Portuguese
   (Mozambican/Angolan Portuguese in particular).
