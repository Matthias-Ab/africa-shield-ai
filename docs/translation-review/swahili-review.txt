AFRICA SHIELD AI — SWAHILI TRANSLATION REVIEW
================================================

STATUS: CONFIRMED CORRECT by a native Swahili speaker (2026-08-17).
Source file: backend/app/models/translations.py (lines ~100-104).
Used for: SMS alerts, USSD "check flood risk" reply, and voice call
text-to-speech (spoken aloud when someone answers a voice alert).

Demo city used below: Nairobi, Kenya. (Swahili is also used for
Tanzania and Uganda — same 3 sentences, just a different city name
substituted in.)

--------------------------------------------------
1. HIGH RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Swahili (as sent):
Hatari ya mafuriko ni KUBWA katika Nairobi. Hamia mahali salama na
epuka kingo za mto.

Intended meaning (English gloss, for reference only):
"Flood risk is HIGH in Nairobi. Move to a safe place and avoid
riverbanks."

--------------------------------------------------
2. MEDIUM RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Swahili (as sent):
Hatari ya mafuriko ni WASTANI katika Nairobi. Kuwa macho na fuatilia
taarifa za eneo lako.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is MODERATE in Nairobi. Be alert and follow updates for
your area."

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOW RISK
--------------------------------------------------
Swahili (as sent):
Hatari ya mafuriko ni NDOGO katika Nairobi. Hakuna hatua inayohitajika
kwa sasa.

Intended meaning (English gloss):
"Flood risk is LOW in Nairobi. No action is needed at this time."


REVIEW OUTCOME
================
All 3 messages confirmed correct, including for reuse across Kenya,
Tanzania, and Uganda. City names (Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Kampala) stay
in their existing form — Swahili does not rename these cities, so no
localized-name change was needed here (unlike Arabic/Somali).
